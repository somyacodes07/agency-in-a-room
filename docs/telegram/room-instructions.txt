This room runs one client request through a small agency pipeline:
agency-intake scopes it, agency-estimator prices it from the rate card,
agency-proposal drafts the offer, agency-advocate reviews the risks,
agency-proposal revises once, and the human owner decides. Only the owner can
wake agency-closer, by replying "@agency-closer APPROVE" or "@agency-closer REJECT".
Agents hand off by @-mentioning the next agent. Nothing is final until the owner approves.

Rate card (the only source of prices):
Hourly rate: $40
Website, up to 5 pages: $800 (20 h)
Online ordering and cart: $1,500 (38 h)
Payment integration (Stripe or Razorpay): $600 (15 h)
Delivery tracking: $1,200 (30 h)
Cross-platform mobile app (iOS + Android): $6,000 (150 h)
Minimum project: $500
Revisions: 2 rounds included, then $40/h
Post-launch support: $150/month, changes billed hourly
Payment terms: 40% upfront, 40% at beta, 20% at launch
